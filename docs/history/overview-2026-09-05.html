Local verification PASS · 5 September 2026

Keep a database compromise from authorizing a transfer.

The project adds verifiable transaction integrity to an existing relational application. The target is specific: forged or altered records in the primary database must not cause unauthorized settlement while the application, integrity service, independent audit evidence and protected settlement store remain trusted.

VIEW 2 · FULL PRODUCTION TARGET — INFRASTRUCTURE NOT DEPLOYED

Independent evidence, an exact check and protected settlement

The primary DB is the attacker’s domain. Services must not infer settlement permission from its status flags or the mere presence of a verification row. The host application owns user/business authorization; the integrity module authenticates its trusted service callers. Production adds independently administered IAM/accounts, non-exportable KMS/HSM custody, protected retention, TLS and recovery operations beyond the local implementation.

Bind the exact operation

A byte-exact, versioned envelope covers operation identity, accounts, integer amount/currency, precise time and correction references. The key service commits independent issuance evidence before returning the HMAC. Only the trusted application may request issuance.

A missing issued row creates an anomaly to reconcile; it does not prove whether the cause was failure, delay or deletion.

Settle the checked snapshot

The processor compares the current canonical hash with independent verified evidence and executes that same snapshot. The protected ledger owns authoritative balances/holds and atomically records postings, execution uniqueness and a durable outcome outbox.

Outcome delivery and primary status updates are eventual. Idempotent retries and reconciliation recover delays without a distributed ACID transaction or duplicate settlement.

LOCAL HISTORY CODE + PRODUCTION RETENTION TARGET

Retain a verifiable history outside the primary database

HMAC checks individual operations internally. Signed Merkle checkpoints support external public-key verification of history. The local code uses receipt inventory, ordered audit events, prefix/root validation and signed heads retained outside PostgreSQL. Independent witnesses and a full external consistency/freshness protocol remain production work. A root saved only beside its data can be rolled back with it.

WORM where required, backups/PITR and restore testing are complementary production controls. The design does not claim regulatory compliance or prove the truth of real-world business inputs.

What a technical presentation must make clear

Integration scope

The proposed contribution combines established cryptography, independent evidence and protected settlement for existing relational applications. It does not claim a new cryptographic algorithm.

User/business authorization belongs to the integrating application. The integrity module requires authenticated service access to MAC issuance, not its own end-user identity platform or mandatory actor fields.

Operating policy and limits

Invalid operations must be quarantined without moving value. A failed HMAC does not establish the recipient’s guilt. Automatic account blocking remains an unresolved policy decision.

Protected simulated funding passed the live PostgreSQL harness; corrections and reversals have code/test coverage. Check the status document for the precise evidence and remaining gates. Real deployments also need trustworthy external funding evidence, independently administered key/evidence stores and operational recovery controls.
